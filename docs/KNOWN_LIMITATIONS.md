# Known Limitations and Demo Boundaries

## Purpose
This document identifies current limits before a command demonstration or pilot.

## Demo boundaries
- Use sample data only.
- Do not use real reports, victims, suspects, evidence, or operational records.
- Do not present the system as approved for production use until reviewed.

## Current focus areas
- Mobile report workflow stability.
- Facts and narrative page reliability.
- Scanner and camera fallback behavior.
- Law lookup result relevance.
- Dashboard polish.
- Light and dark mode readability.
- Role-specific navigation consistency.

## AI limitations
AI features support drafting and summarization only.
AI output must be reviewed by an officer or supervisor before use.
AI must not make enforcement decisions, provide final legal advice, or invent facts.

## CLEOC limitation
The portal does not replace CLEOC. It supports the field-to-report workflow by organizing typed information, suggested forms, and draft narrative content before CLEOC entry.

## Production limitations
Before operational use, the following require review:
- Hosting location.
- CAC or approved authentication method.
- Records retention.
- PII handling.
- Audit logging.
- Access controls.
- ISSM and G6 review.
- Command approval.
