# ISSM / G6 Review Notes

## Purpose
This document summarizes major technical and operational areas likely to require review before pilot or production deployment.

## Current positioning
The MCPD Portal is currently being prepared for controlled demonstration and proof-of-concept review.

The portal should not be considered approved for operational deployment until reviewed through appropriate channels.

## Major review areas

### Authentication and access control
- Role-based access structure exists.
- CAC integration planning exists.
- User/session security review required.

### Hosting
- Hosting environment must be reviewed and approved.
- HTTPS enforcement required.
- Government-approved deployment architecture may be required.

### PII handling
- Real operational records should not be used in demonstrations.
- PII storage and retention policy review required.
- Audit and access logging review required.

### Mobile support
- Mobile-first workflows are included.
- Camera/scanner permissions and browser policy review required.
- Offline/PWA behavior requires review.

### AI-assisted features
- AI intended for draft assistance only.
- Human review required.
- AI should not replace official reporting systems or legal review.

### Data retention
- Record retention and deletion policies require review.
- Backup and recovery planning required.

### Integration boundaries
- The portal supports workflows leading into CLEOC.
- The portal is not positioned as a CLEOC replacement.

## Recommended next step
Recommended path:
1. Controlled leadership demonstration.
2. Internal proof-of-concept feedback.
3. Workflow stabilization.
4. ISSM/G6 consultation.
5. Pilot planning discussion.
