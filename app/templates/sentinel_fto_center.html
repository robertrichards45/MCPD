Program Roadmap

Standard and Accelerated Paths

The roadmap is a training framework. The approved MCPD FTO order/curriculum remains the controlling source.

FTO / Supervisor controlled

Standard FTO Program — 8 Weeks

  1. Orientation & Foundations — department procedures, radio, safety, and report baseline.
  2. Patrol & Calls for Service — response, scene organization, interviews, and documentation decisions.
  3. Traffic & Access Control — vehicle contacts, access-control incidents, and documentation.
  4. Investigations & Evidence — statements, property/evidence, report packets, and investigative follow-up.
  5. Legal Articulation — connect enforcement decisions to facts and applicable authority.
  6. Complex Calls — multi-party incidents and higher-complexity documentation.
  7. Independent Operations — trainee performs the primary role with reduced prompting.
  8. Final Evaluation — outstanding curriculum, remediation, report quality, and recommendation.

Accelerated FTO Program — 4 Weeks

  1. Installation Orientation — local orders, procedures, geography/posts, radio, and reporting expectations.
  2. Patrol & Enforcement Validation — demonstrate core field skills and legal/report articulation.
  3. Complex Incident Validation — demonstrate complete incident handling and paperwork decisions.
  4. Independent Validation — final performance review, policy knowledge, remediation closure, and recommendation.
Important: accelerated training should not automatically waive installation-specific requirements. Any shortened program should follow the approved FTO policy and supervisory authorization.

DOR & Remediation Workflow

Observation → FTO Review → DOR → Acknowledgment → Development

1. Observe

Record what the FTO actually observed during the shift or training event.

2. Evaluate

Use consistent categories and approved standards. Sentinel may organize cues but does not set the final rating.

3. Document

The FTO finalizes the DOR comments, ratings, remediation, and required follow-up.

4. Develop

Track improvement, repeat observation where needed, and close remediation only after the FTO verifies performance.
