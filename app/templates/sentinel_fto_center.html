Program Roadmap

Standard and Accelerated Paths

This is a working framework. The approved MCPD FTO order/curriculum remains the controlling source.

Standard FTO Program — 8 Weeks

  1. Orientation & Foundations — procedures, radio, safety, reporting baseline.
  2. Patrol & Calls for Service — response, scene organization, interviews, documentation.
  3. Traffic & Access Control — vehicle contacts and installation access incidents.
  4. Investigations & Evidence — statements, property/evidence, report packets.
  5. Legal Articulation — connect enforcement decisions to observed facts and authority.
  6. Complex Calls — multi-party incidents and higher-complexity documentation.
  7. Independent Operations — reduced prompting with direct FTO observation.
  8. Final Evaluation — outstanding requirements, remediation, and supervisory recommendation.

Accelerated FTO Program — 4 Weeks

  1. Installation Orientation — local orders, geography/posts, radio, and reporting expectations.
  2. Patrol & Enforcement Validation — core field skills and legal/report articulation.
  3. Complex Incident Validation — complete incident handling and paperwork decisions.
  4. Independent Validation — final performance review, remediation closure, and recommendation.
Accelerated training does not automatically waive installation-specific requirements. Supervisory authorization and the approved FTO policy control.
DOR & Remediation Workflow

Observe → Evaluate → Finalize → Review → Develop

The official Program Dashboard turns this workflow into persistent trainee records while keeping every employment/training decision with authorized personnel.

1. Observe
Record only performance actually observed.
2. Evaluate
The FTO enters the official DOR ratings and comments.
3. Review
Supervisor review and trainee acknowledgment are separately recorded.
4. Develop
Remediation stays open until an authorized human verifies performance.
