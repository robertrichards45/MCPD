Which workflow do I need?

Routine crash scene: use the Officer Diagram. Complex/fatal/technical reconstruction: use Accident Reconstruction if authorized.

Officer

Officer Accident Diagram

For the responding officer. Build a clear scene diagram that supports the report packet without turning the tool into a reconstruction opinion.

  • Drag-and-drop vehicles, signs, roadway objects, and impact markers
  • Numbered waypoints to show sequence or final positions
  • Callout notes for observations and measured facts
  • Traffic controls, skid/yaw marks, debris, and points of impact
  • Export PNG for the case packet
Do not estimate as fact: speeds, causation, measurements, or impact sequence must be identified as observed, measured, reported, or estimated.

Investigator

Accident Reconstruction

For trained/authorized accident investigators. Use when the case requires technical measurements, calculations, evidence management, or a reconstruction packet.

  • Officer diagram features plus precise measurements
  • Skid length, yaw radius, impact point, and roadway measurements
  • Photo, video, and PDF evidence attachment
  • Multi-vehicle records, timeline, and investigator notes
  • Downloadable reconstruction packet PDF
Verification required: calculations and reconstruction conclusions must be reviewed by trained personnel before being treated as investigative findings.
